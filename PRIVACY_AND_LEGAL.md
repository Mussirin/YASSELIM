# YASSELIM – Privacy Policy, Legal Terms & Store Disclosures

## 1. Privacy Policy & Local Data Handling
- **100% On-Device Personal Writings**: All journal entries, moods, tags, and unsent letters created in **YASSELIM** are stored exclusively on the user's local device.
- **No Account, No Cloud, No AI Training**: YASSELIM does not require user registration, does not synchronize personal writings to external cloud servers, and never processes personal writings with artificial intelligence or remote analytics.
- **Encryption at Rest**: Personal entries and unsent letters are encrypted locally at rest using SHA-256 key derivation, counter-mode stream encryption, and HMAC-SHA256 authentication prior to persistence.
- **User Control (KVKK & GDPR Compliance)**: Users can export all of their data in portable JSON format or permanently erase all local records at any time from **Settings > Delete All Local Data**.

## 2. Medical & Psychological Disclaimer
- **Not a Medical or Therapy Product**: YASSELIM is strictly a personal writing and journaling application.
- It does **NOT** provide psychological therapy, clinical counseling, psychiatric evaluation, medical diagnosis, medical treatment, or emergency crisis assistance.
- If you are experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, please contact your local emergency services or a licensed healthcare professional immediately.

## 3. Advertising & One-Time Premium Purchase Disclosure
- **Free Version with Contextual Banner Area**: In the free edition, a limited, non-intrusive sponsor area is shown only on non-writing screens when internet connectivity is available.
- **Zero Ads in Writing Editor**: Advertisements are **never** rendered inside the Writing Editor screen, and private journal text is **never** shared with advertising networks.
- **YASSELIM Premium (One-Time Purchase)**: Users may optionally upgrade to YASSELIM Premium via a single one-time payment (no recurring subscription) to permanently remove all advertisements while retaining full access to all features.
